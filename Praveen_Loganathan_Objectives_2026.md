# Objectives — Lead AI Engineer, AI & Automation Platform
**Praveen Loganathan**

---

## Commercial Mindset

**Objective:** Help direct engineering effort towards opportunities with real, evidenced value, and build in ways that lower the cost of future delivery for the team.

- I will contribute engineering and commercial assessment to proposed use cases — whether the value is measurable, whether AI is the right instrument, and whether the process should be simplified before anything is built.
- I will constructively challenge or recommend deferral where value, feasibility or supportability is unclear, and share the reasoning so decisions are transparent to the team and the business.
- I will work with the team to establish reusable delivery patterns and reference architectures — RAG over policy content, document extraction, agentic workflow — so future use cases start from a proven baseline.
- I will establish cost visibility per use case, covering inference, infrastructure and support, so investment decisions can be made against cost per outcome.

**Measures**
- Assessment approach agreed and in use, with at least one proposal materially reshaped or stopped on feasibility or commercial grounds.
- At least two reusable patterns published with the team and adopted by a later use case.
- Cost model established and reported for the first production delivery.

---

## Customer Journeys

**Objective:** Build understanding of end-to-end processes by working alongside the business, so what we build addresses the real constraint rather than the requested one.

- I will work alongside the business areas we support rather than at arm's length, using rapid wireframes and working prototypes to test direction with users early and correct course while it is still cheap to do so.
- I will iterate in short, visible cycles with design, journey optimisation and delivery colleagues, with regular show-and-tell sessions so direction is validated against real reactions rather than documented requirements.
- I will run discovery with Operations, Customer Journeys and front office colleagues to understand where effort, delay and risk actually sit.
- I will ensure the initiatives I work on have a named business owner, a defined outcome and an agreed measure before build begins.
- I will bring realistic engineering judgement into use-case discussions on what current AI capability can and cannot do reliably, including where the better answer is process redesign, integration or rules.

**Measures**
- Discovery completed with at least three business areas, findings shared with the platform team and a prioritised opportunity view produced.
- Prototype or wireframe review held with users and design colleagues before build commences on each initiative I lead.
- Documented outcome measure and business owner in place at build approval for initiatives I lead.

## Engineering Quality

**Objective:** Work with the team, risk and compliance to define what production-ready means for AI systems specifically, and deliver to that standard.

- I will develop and publish the AI quality and evaluation standard with input from risk and compliance: how a use case defines "good enough" before build, how retrieval and output quality are measured against ground truth, how regression is detected as prompts, models and content change, and the bar for production.
- I will ensure solutions are assessed for usability and trust alongside technical accuracy — how sources, confidence and correction paths are surfaced to users — since these determine whether an AI solution is relied on in practice.
- I will support colleagues in applying that standard to in-flight work, so quality is assessed against a shared definition rather than individual judgement.
- I will build evaluation and observability as shared platform capabilities rather than per-project effort — logging, groundedness and accuracy measurement, drift monitoring — giving risk, compliance and audit a single evidence base.
- I will apply AI-assisted development practices in delivery, including Copilot for test generation, documentation and code review, and share what proves effective with the team.

**Measures**
- AI quality and evaluation standard published, agreed with risk and compliance, and covering usability and trust as well as output accuracy.
- Standard operating as a pre-production gate, with a shared evaluation harness adopted across in-flight AI work.
- In-flight work assessed against the standard, with gaps and a remediation path agreed with the relevant engineers.

## Productivity & High Performance Culture

**Objective:** Strengthen the team's collective capability in applied AI engineering and ensure our solutions are supportable by more than one person.

- I will ensure every solution I contribute to can be supported and changed by at least two engineers, through architecture decision records covering the non-obvious design choices, operational runbooks, and a named second engineer with hands-on familiarity.
- I will bring engineers directly into evaluation, retrieval design and tuning work, so these become team capabilities rather than specialist ones.
- I will support colleagues through pairing, design review and knowledge sharing on applied AI engineering practice.
- I will trial emerging tooling with the team and share evidenced findings, so adoption is decided on results rather than novelty.

**Measures**
- Every solution taken to production has a named second supporter who has independently handled a change or incident on it.
- Enablement sessions delivered to the team on evaluation and applied AI engineering.
- Architecture decision records and runbooks maintained for work I contribute to.

---

## Simplification & Automation

**Objective:** Help the platform land its first production-grade AI solution, and build it as the pattern the team reuses.

- I will lead delivery of the first production-grade AI solution end to end, with a defined business outcome and audit-ready controls.
- I will define a genuinely shippable MVP for each initiative and extend it based on observed use, so scope is set by what people rely on rather than by specification written ahead of contact with users.
- I will work with the central AI function and business owners to take use cases through governance, engineering delivery and into operational adoption, ensuring transition into live running is planned rather than assumed.
- I will ensure agreed benefits are measured and evidenced after implementation, including where realised benefit differs from the business case.
- I will build the first delivery deliberately as a reusable pattern — architecture, controls, evaluation approach and support model — so the team's subsequent use cases are materially faster.

**Measures**
- First production-grade AI solution live, adopted by its intended users, with benefits evidenced post-implementation.
- Benefits realisation review completed and shared.
- Demonstrable reduction in elapsed delivery time for the second use case relative to the first.

## T&T Strategy Delivery

**Objective:** Provide engineering substance behind the platform's delivery commitments, and help establish the operating model and technical direction that make them repeatable.

- I will help define and pilot a forward-deployed engineering operating model — engagement shape and duration, the graduation bar from prototype to supported production service, and handover and support arrangements — so proximity to the business does not come at the cost of supportability.
- I will deliver against the commitments I make and raise variance in advance rather than reporting it afterwards.
- I will provide engineering input into the central AI function's governance and prioritisation on feasibility, supportability, cost and risk, so decisions rest on an accurate technical picture.
- I will contribute a technical position on build versus buy — where Copilot and vendor capability are the right answer and where platform-built solutions earn their cost — and a capability roadmap sequenced against forming business demand.

**Measures**
- Operating model piloted on at least one use case, with engagement and graduation approach documented and reviewed with the team.
- Build-versus-buy position and capability roadmap documented and reviewed with the central AI function.
- Delivery commitments met, with variances flagged in advance.
- Engineering input evidenced in at least one governance, prioritisation or technology direction decision.
